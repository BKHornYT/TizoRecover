Looking for drives…

Quick scan

Reads the drive's own file table. Takes seconds to minutes, and finds deleted files with their real names and folders, as long as the table still remembers them.

Deep scan

Does a quick scan, then reads every free byte and recognises files by their contents. Slower (minutes per 10 GB), but finds photos, videos and documents even after a format. Names are lost for those.

Fix a drive

Drive not showing up? TizoRecover checks every step from the plug to a drive letter and fixes what it safely can. It never formats or initializes anything.

Watch while I plug it in

The best test: TizoRecover watches every USB port while you connect the drive and tells you where it gets stuck.

  1. Unplug the drive.
  2. Press Start watching.
  3. Plug it in, straight into the PC if you can (no adapters or hubs).

Check my drives

Erase a removable drive
